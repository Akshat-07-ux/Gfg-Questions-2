<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/snake-case0219/1">Snake Case</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a sentence <strong>s</strong> consisting of English alphabet characters and spaces, convert it into snake_case.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">In snake case:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><span style="font-size: 18px;">All letters are converted to lowercase.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 18px;">Words are separated by a single underscore ('_') instead of spaces.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 18px;">No spaces should appear in the resulting string.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Return the snake_case representation of the given sentence.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Note:</strong> The input sentence does not begin with a whitespace character.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples: </strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> s<strong> = </strong>"Geeks ForGeeks"
<strong>Output: </strong>"geeks_forgeeks"
<strong>Explanation: </strong>All upper case characters are converted to lower case and the whitespace characters are replaced with underscore '_'.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> s<strong> = </strong>"Here comes the garden"
<strong>Output: </strong>"here_comes_the_garden"</span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Explanation:</strong> All upper case characters are converted to lower case and the whitespace characters are replaced with underscore '_'.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>1 ≤ n ≤ 10<sup>5</sup>, n is length of the sentence</span></p></div><br><p><span style=font-size:18px><strong>Topic Tags : </strong><br><code>Strings</code>&nbsp;
